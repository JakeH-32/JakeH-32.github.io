Christmas Gifts for Husband

Husbands are often hard to shop for because they buy what they need when they need it. The gifts that work best are better versions of things he already uses, a treat he'd skip for himself, or something you'll do together.

The picks go in price order, from Under $25 through $25–50 and $50–100, ending with two splurges over $100. Each one names the kind of husband it suits, from the griller and the gym regular to the one who'd rather be home playing a board game.

How we chose these picks

We focused on brands with long track records and years of consistent owner feedback (Opinel, Smartwool, Kiehl's, JBL, MEATER, Hyperice) and read the complaints to find each item's honest trade-off. We kept clothing to items that are forgiving on size.

We left out gag gifts and 'world's best husband' novelties. Everything here is something he'll reach for long after Christmas.

We list rough price bands instead of exact prices because prices on Amazon change constantly. Tap any button to see today's price. Price mix in this guide: Under $25: 4 · $25–50: 3 · $50–100: 3 · $100+: 2.

Tips for shopping for your husband

  • Look at what he uses until it's worn out: slippers, socks, the knife in his junk drawer. Upgrade that.
  • Add a plan to any gift: a date night from the scratch-off book or a first game of Ticket to Ride on Christmas afternoon.
  • Keep receipts for anything with a size. Slippers are the most common exchange.
