Christmas Gifts for Grandpa

Christmas gifts for grandpa built around how he spends his days: watching birds, listening to the game, whittling on the porch or teaching the grandkids a game.

Grandpas tend to have settled habits and a garage full of tools, so the best gifts support the hobbies he already loves or give him a reason to spend time with the grandkids. A good bird feeder, a dependable radio or a game the whole family can play goes a long way.

Picks are ordered by price, from Under $25 through $25–50 and $50–100, with two bigger gifts over $100 that work well as a joint gift from the family.

How we chose these picks

We focused on brands with long track records and years of consistent owner feedback (Sony, Zippo, Flexcut, Titleist, Brome, Celestron) and favored simple controls, clear displays and sturdy builds. We read the critical reviews for problems like small buttons and hard-to-read screens.

Many of these gifts are easy for a grandchild to share in, like a game of dominoes or a morning of birdwatching together.

Tips for shopping for grandpa

  • Pair a gift with time: a game of dominoes, a morning at the bird feeder, or an afternoon filling in his story journal with a grandchild.
  • Choose simple controls and large displays. He'll use what's easy to use.
  • For a bigger gift like binoculars, consider going in on it together as a family.
