Christmas Gifts for Brother

Christmas gifts for your brother, whether he's the gamer, the gym regular, the reader or the one who just moved into his first place.

Brothers are easy to shop for once you know which kind you've got. Some want something for the game they play every night, some want gear for the gym or the trail, and some just moved and own two forks. This list covers all of them.

Picks run in price order, from Under $15 and Under $25 through $25–50 and $50–100. Most are fine for a younger brother in college as well as an older one with his own family.

How we chose these picks

We focused on well-known brands with years of consistent owner feedback (Hasbro, Nalgene, Logitech, Catan, Osprey, Weber) and read the critical reviews so each pick has an honest caveat. We avoided clothing that depends on exact size.

Several picks are games, on purpose. Siblings tend to remember a holiday afternoon spent playing something together for years.

We list rough price bands instead of exact prices because prices on Amazon change constantly. Tap any button to see today's price. Price mix in this guide: Under $15: 2 · Under $25: 4 · $25–50: 4 · $50–100: 2.

Monopoly Deal Card Game

Under $15Best for: the brother who's always up for a quick game at family gatherings

Monopoly Deal keeps the property trading and rent-charging of Monopoly but plays in about 15 minutes with a single deck. It's cheap, fits in a pocket, and gets surprisingly competitive.

Tips for shopping for your brother

  • Ask his partner or roommate what he's been talking about lately. Siblings often don't say what they want to each other.
  • Games make good sibling gifts because you get to play them together on Christmas Day.
  • If he just moved, practical things for his place get used more than anything else.
