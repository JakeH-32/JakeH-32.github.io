White Elephant Gifts Under $25

The best white elephant gifts are funny and secretly useful. These picks get a laugh when they're opened and a steal before the game ends.

Curated by the Sprig & Bow team · Updated October 2026 · 11 picks

See the 11 picks Save to Pinterest (opens in a new tab)

A great white elephant gift hits two notes at once: it gets a laugh when it's unwrapped, and then people realize they actually want it. Pure gag gifts get left behind; boring gifts get forgotten.

Every pick here usually sells for under $25, works for a mixed crowd (coworkers, in-laws, friends of friends), and is safe for an office party.

How we chose these picks

We focused on long-time gift-swap favorites with broad appeal and lots of positive owner feedback: small kitchen gadgets, party games and a few harmless novelty items. We avoided anything crude, anything that needs sizing, and anything that only one type of person would want.

Party games are especially strong picks, because the winner can open them and play on the spot.

White elephant tips

  • Stick to the budget. A gift far above the limit makes others feel awkward.
  • Wrap it in a misleading box. Half the fun is the reveal.
  • Practical-plus-funny wins: the most-stolen gifts are usually small kitchen gadgets and games.
