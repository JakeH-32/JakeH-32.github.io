Gifts for Teens Under $50

Headphones, room lights, games and a few things they've been asking for, all usually under $50 and picked for teens 13 to 17.

Teenagers are hard to shop for because their tastes change fast and they notice when a gift misses. The safest bets are things they use every day: headphones, a speaker for their room, a water bottle they carry to school, or a game to play with friends.

Every pick here usually sells for under $50. Most work for any teen from 13 to 17, and each note says who it suits best.

How we chose these picks

We started with what teens ask for, using wish lists, parent forums and best-seller rankings, then narrowed the list to products from established brands with a long record of strong owner feedback (JBL, LEGO, Crocs, Fujifilm, Owala). We read the critical reviews for each one to find the common complaints.

We left out anything that depends on a specific phone or console, and anything that needs exact clothing sizes. Crocs are the one exception, since they come in simple whole sizes.

We list rough price bands instead of exact prices because prices on Amazon change constantly. Tap any button to see today's price. Price mix in this guide: Under $15: 2 · Under $25: 3 · $25–50: 7.

JBL Go 4 Portable Bluetooth Speaker (Black)

$25–50Best for: teens who play music in their room, the shower or at friends' houses

The Go 4 is about the size of a phone wallet, survives drops and splashes with an IP67 rating, and sounds fuller than its size suggests. Two Go 4 speakers can pair up for stereo, so a second one makes an easy birthday gift later.

Worth knowing: Battery life is around seven hours, so it needs regular charging. Bass is limited at this size.

JBL Tune 520BT Wireless On-Ear Headphones

$25–50Best for: the teen who keeps losing or breaking earbuds

The Tune 520BT is light, folds flat for a backpack, and JBL rates the battery at up to 57 hours. It connects to two devices at once, so switching between a phone and a laptop for homework is easy.

Worth knowing: There's no active noise cancelling, and on-ear pads can get uncomfortable for some people after a few hours. Amazon also lists renewed units, so check that you're buying new.

Tips for shopping for teens

  • Ask a sibling, cousin or friend what they've been talking about. Teens rarely tell adults, and their friends always know.
  • A gift card to a store they already shop at is fine, especially paired with something small to open.
  • For anything with sizes or colors, include a gift receipt.
