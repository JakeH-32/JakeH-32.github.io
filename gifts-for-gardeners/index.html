Gifts for Gardeners

Pruners, trowels, gloves and a few winter projects for the person whose favorite place is the garden bed.

Curated by the Sprig & Bow team · Updated October 2026 · 12 picks

See the 12 picks Save to Pinterest (opens in a new tab)

Most gardeners already own a shed full of tools, but they also have favorites, and they wear tools out. The best gifts are better versions of what they reach for every day: pruners, a digging knife, gloves and a good way to water.

The holidays fall in the off-season for most of North America, so we also included a few things for winter: a seed-starting heat mat, a planning book and hand cream for the dry months.

How we chose these picks

We looked for tools that come up again and again in gardening forums, extension-service advice and long-term owner reviews, from makers with a long track record (Felco, Fiskars, Nisaku, Dramm). Then we read the critical reviews to find out what wears out, breaks or disappoints.

Most picks suit any kind of gardener. Where one fits a particular type, such as vegetable growers or people with roses and shrubs, the note says so.

Felco F-2 Classic Manual Hand Pruner

$50–100Best for: serious gardeners with roses, fruit trees or shrubs

The Felco 2 is the Swiss-made pruner that many professional landscapers and public gardens use, and it's built to last for decades. Every part can be replaced, from the blade to the spring, so it can be repaired for life.

Worth knowing: It's sized for medium to large hands, and left-handed gardeners need the Felco 9. It's a splurge for someone who only trims a few plants.

Tips for shopping for gardeners

  • Look at what's worn out by the back door: cracked gloves, a rusty trowel or a leaky nozzle are easy upgrades.
  • Find out whether they grow vegetables, flowers or houseplants. The best gift depends on which.
  • Pair a tool with seed packets from a regional seed company or a gift card to a nearby nursery.
