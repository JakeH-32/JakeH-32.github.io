Gifts for People Who Have Everything

For the person who says 'I don't need anything': little luxuries, shared experiences and clever upgrades they'd never buy for themselves.

When someone truly has everything they need, the best gifts fall into three buckets: an upgraded version of something they use every day, a consumable luxury they wouldn't splurge on, or something that creates a shared experience or memory.

Every pick below fits at least one of those buckets. The list skews a little higher in price, but there are options in several bands.

How we chose these picks

We avoided gadgets that add clutter and looked for things with staying power: products from established brands with strong long-term owner feedback, and consumables that get used up. We also favored gifts with a social side (photos, games, cooking) because shared time is the one thing people rarely have enough of.

For a few items, such as cocktail smoker kits, there's no single dominant brand, so we've linked to a search to compare options.

How to shop for someone who has everything

  • Upgrade, don't add: a nicer version of something they use daily beats a new gadget.
  • Consumables are your friend. Good oil, skincare or hot sauce gets used up and never clutters a shelf.
  • Add your time. Preloading the photo frame or planning a game night makes any gift more meaningful.
