Christmas Gifts for Boyfriend

Christmas gifts for your boyfriend that suit any stage of the relationship, from a two-player game for a new couple to a pair of headphones he'll wear every day.

A good gift for a boyfriend fits two things: who he is and how long you've been together. Early on, you want something useful and fun that doesn't feel like a big statement. Later, you can go bigger on the thing he keeps saying he'll buy one day. Picks marked 'early relationship' are safe at any stage.

The list starts with a small add-on Under $15 and runs through Under $25, $25–50 and $50–100, with two splurges over $100. Each pick names the kind of guy it suits: the gamer, the outdoorsman, the one who's always fixing something, or the one who lives in his headphones.

How we chose these picks

We chose products from brands with long track records and years of steady owner feedback (YETI, Philips, LEGO, Bellroy, Leatherman, Sony) and read the complaints to give each one a fair caveat. We left out most clothing, since sizing is hard to guess.

We also avoided novelty gifts that get a laugh and then sit in a drawer. Everything here is meant to get regular use.

Tips for shopping for your boyfriend

  • Listen for the item he keeps replacing with a cheap version. That's the one to upgrade.
  • For a newer relationship, a shared activity (a game, a fire pit evening) says a lot without feeling like too much.
  • Clothing is the riskiest category. Check the size of something he already wears, and include a gift receipt.
