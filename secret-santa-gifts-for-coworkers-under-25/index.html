Secret Santa Gifts for Coworkers Under $25

Useful, office-friendly gifts for a coworker you barely know: treats, desk upgrades and a few things for the break room, all usually under $25.

Drawing a coworker's name in Secret Santa often means buying for someone you know mainly from meetings. The goal is a gift that's useful at a desk, safe for any workplace, and easy to like without knowing their tastes.

Everything here usually sells for under $25, the most common office limit. We avoided clothing, strong scents and jokes that could land badly with someone you don't know well.

How we chose these picks

We focused on long-time gift-exchange favorites from familiar brands (Ferrero, Contigo, Pilot, Moleskine, Twinings) with plenty of positive owner feedback. We favored things that get used at a desk, in the break room or on the commute.

If you know one detail about your person, like whether they drink coffee or tea, use it to choose between the picks.

Secret Santa tips

  • Ask the organizer whether your person filled out a wish list or mentioned any allergies.
  • Stay close to the price limit. Going far over can make other people feel awkward.
  • Skip personal items like clothing, perfume or anything that comments on someone's habits.
  • Add a handwritten card. It costs nothing, and people remember it.
