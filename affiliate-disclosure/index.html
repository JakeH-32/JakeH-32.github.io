What that means for you

Many links on this site, including every "Check price on Amazon" button, are affiliate links. If you click one and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a small commission. You don't pay anything extra, and the price you see on Amazon is the same either way.

Affiliate links are marked in our code as sponsored, open in a new tab, and point directly to Amazon.com. We don't use link shorteners or cloaked redirects.

Does this affect what we recommend?

We try hard to make sure it doesn't. Commission rates vary by category, but our picks are chosen for the person on your list, and every pick includes a caveat so you can decide for yourself. Brands do not pay to be included.
