Most grandmothers will tell you they don't need a thing, and that the best gift is time with the family. The gifts here lean into that: things that bring the family closer, help with the hobbies she loves, or make everyday life a little more comfortable.

Picks are in price order, from Under $15 and Under $25 through $25–50 and $50–100, with two tech gifts over $100 that are worth splitting between siblings or cousins.

How we chose these picks

We focused on brands with long track records and years of consistent owner feedback (Nordic Ware, Clover, See's Candies, Brightech, Skylight, Amazon) and read the critical reviews for real-world issues like small print, fiddly setup or stiff buttons.

For the tech picks, we favored devices that someone else in the family can set up and manage from their own phone, so grandma gets the benefit without the hassle.

Tips for shopping for grandma

  • The best gifts for grandparents often include the grandkids: a drawing tucked in the box, or a call to help fill in her story journal.
  • For tech gifts, set everything up before you wrap it, and load in some family photos.
  • Ask about her hands, eyes and balance before buying tools, books or slippers, and choose the version that's easiest to use.
