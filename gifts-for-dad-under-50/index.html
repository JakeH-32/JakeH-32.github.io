Gifts for Dad Under $50

For the dad who says 'don't get me anything': rugged, useful gifts that live in his pocket, truck, toolbox or grill station.

Plenty of dads are famously hard to shop for. They'll say they don't need anything, then use the same cracked thermos for fifteen years. The sweet spot is a well-made version of something he already uses, or a tool for the hobby he keeps talking about.

Everything here usually sells for under $50, and nearly all of it is built to take some abuse.

How we chose these picks

We focused on brands with long reputations for durability (Stanley, Carhartt, Fiskars, Darn Tough, Gerber) and products that consistently earn strong long-term owner feedback. We favored everyday-carry and hobby gear over novelty 'dad' gifts.

Each pick says which kind of dad it suits: the griller, the camper, the fixer, or the one who just likes a quiet drink after the kids are asleep.

Tips for shopping for dad

  • Look at what he already uses until it breaks: that's the item to upgrade.
  • Pair a small gift with a plan: the Glencairn glasses with a bottle, the cribbage board with a promised game night.
  • Keep receipts for clothing and socks; sizing is the most common reason for returns.
