Christmas Gifts for Girlfriend

Thoughtful Christmas gifts for your girlfriend, from easy early-relationship picks to a couple of splurges for when you know her well.

Shopping for a girlfriend comes with an extra question: how long have you been together? Two months in, a gift should feel thoughtful and low-pressure. Two years in, you can go more personal or spend more. This list covers both, and the picks marked 'early relationship' are safe at any stage.

The picks run from Under $25, through $25–50 and $50–100, to two splurges over $100. Each one notes the kind of person it suits: the homebody, the style-minded, the one who takes photos of everything, or the one who listens to podcasts on every walk.

How we chose these picks

We stuck to established brands with years of consistent owner feedback (Kitsch, Stanley, LEGO, Fujifilm, Kendra Scott, Barefoot Dreams, Apple) and read the critical reviews to find each pick's real trade-off. We left out anything that depends on knowing her exact size.

We also skipped gifts that hint at self-improvement. Nobody wants a scale or a planner full of goals from a partner on Christmas morning.

Tips for shopping for your girlfriend

  • Early in a relationship, choose something she'll use often and pair it with a plan, like the LEGO bouquet and a night in building it.
  • Notice what she already reaches for: the scent she wears, the metal of her jewelry, the color of her phone case.
  • Write a card. A few honest lines often mean more to her than the gift itself.
