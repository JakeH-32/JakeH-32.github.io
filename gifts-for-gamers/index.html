Gifts for Gamers

Headsets, controllers, chargers and comfort upgrades for people who play on PC, PlayStation, Xbox or Switch.

Curated by the Sprig & Bow team · Updated October 2026 · 12 picks

See the 12 picks Save to Pinterest (opens in a new tab)

Buying a game for a gamer is risky. They may already own it, prefer digital copies, or play on a different system than you think. Accessories are a safer bet, and the right one gets used every time they sit down to play.

This guide covers headsets, mice and controllers first, then a few comfort upgrades for long sessions. Each pick says which platform it works with, so check what they play on before you buy.

How we chose these picks

We focused on accessories from established gaming brands (HyperX, Logitech, SteelSeries, 8BitDo, BenQ) that show up repeatedly in enthusiast forums and long-term owner reviews. We also read the critical reviews for each product to find common failure points, like peeling ear pads or drifting sticks.

We left out consoles and games, since those depend on what they already own.

We list rough price bands instead of exact prices because prices on Amazon change constantly. Tap any button to see today's price. Price mix in this guide: Under $25: 4 · $25–50: 6 · $50–100: 1 · $100+: 1.

HyperX Cloud III Wired Gaming Headset

The Cloud line has been a top headset recommendation for years because it's comfortable for long sessions and sounds good for the price. The Cloud III connects by 3.5mm, USB-C or USB-A, so it works with PC, PlayStation, Xbox controllers and Switch.

Worth knowing: It's wired, which some players find limiting. This link is for the black-and-red color; HyperX also sells all-black and wireless versions.

Logitech G502 HERO Wired Gaming Mouse

$25–50Best for: PC gamers who want lots of programmable buttons

The G502 has been one of the most popular gaming mice for years, thanks to its thumb rest, 11 programmable buttons and removable weights for tuning the feel. The scroll wheel can switch to free-spinning for fast scrolling.

Worth knowing: It's big and fairly heavy, so it suits medium to large hands. It's wired; the G502 X Lightspeed is the wireless version and costs more.

SteelSeries QcK XXL Gaming Mouse Pad

A desk-size mouse pad gives plenty of room for wide mouse movements and sits under the keyboard too, which keeps everything from sliding around. SteelSeries QcK cloth pads have been a standard among PC players for many years.

Worth knowing: Measure the desk first, since the XXL is about three feet wide. Cloth pads can be hand-washed, but they take a while to dry.

Gamer gifting tips

  • Find out the platform first: PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Switch or phone. Many accessories only work with one.
  • Look at their setup. A worn mouse pad, a headset with flaking ear pads or a dead controller is an easy upgrade.
  • Skip specific games unless you're sure they don't own them, since many people buy digitally.
