Cozy Gifts for Homebodies

Blankets, slippers, a good book, something warm to sip: everything for the person whose favorite plan is no plans at all.

Curated by the Sprig & Bow team · Updated October 2026 · 13 picks

See the 13 picks Save to Pinterest (opens in a new tab)

Some people's idea of a perfect winter evening is a blanket, a hot drink and absolutely nowhere to be. Shopping for them is a pleasure, because coziness is something almost everyone wants more of.

This guide builds a whole night in: something soft to wrap up in, something warm to drink, something to do, and a few splurges that make home feel like a retreat. Mix and match a few small picks to create a cozy gift basket.

How we chose these picks

We prioritized comfort items with long track records and large numbers of happy owners, such as Bedsure throws, UGG slippers and Harney & Sons tea, then checked the common complaints about each (shedding, sizing, scent strength) to write honest caveats.

We included a range from small add-ons to a couple of splurges, so you can build a basket at almost any budget.

YnM Weighted Blanket

$50–100Best for: anxious sleepers and people who love feeling tucked in

Many people find the even pressure of a weighted blanket calming, and YnM is one of the long-running, well-reviewed brands. The glass-bead filling is distributed in small pockets to keep the weight even.

Worth knowing: Choose a weight around 10% of the person's body weight, and note that weighted blankets can feel warm. Not suitable for young children.

How to build a cozy gift basket

  • Start with a throw blanket as the 'basket' and roll the rest inside it.
  • Add one thing to sip (tea or cocoa), one thing to do (a book or puzzle) and one sensory treat (a candle or socks).
  • For a no-spend extra, include a handwritten 'coupon' for a movie night you'll host.
