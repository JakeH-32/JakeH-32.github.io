About Sprig & Bow

Sprig & Bow is a small, independent site that publishes holiday gift guides. Each guide is built around a specific kind of person (a home cook, a coffee lover, a new homeowner) and a realistic budget.

How we choose products

We do not run a product lab, and we won't pretend we do. Our picks come from research: we look for established brands with a long, consistent record of strong owner feedback, read the critical reviews as closely as the glowing ones, and favor things that are easy to buy, easy to return, and likely to still be available next year.

For every pick we write down who it's for, why it makes a good gift, and one honest caveat. If we can't name a specific person a product would delight, it doesn't go in a guide.

What we don't do

  • We don't publish star ratings or "reviews" we didn't earn.
  • We don't list exact prices, because they change constantly. You'll see rough price bands instead.
  • We don't accept payment from brands to include products.

How the site is funded

We earn a commission when you buy through some of our links. See the affiliate disclosure for details. We also run a small Etsy shop selling printable holiday planners and party games, which we mention in a few relevant guides.

Contact

Spotted a broken link or a product that's gone downhill? Email us at the contact address on our Pinterest profile and we'll take a look.
