Christmas Gifts for Sister

Christmas gifts for your sister, from small self-care treats to a crochet kit, a travel book and an instant camera for the one who documents everything.

Sisters are often the people who know us best, which makes the bar for a gift a little higher. The good news is that a sister gift can be small and still land well, as long as it fits who she is: the self-care fan, the crafter, the traveler or the one who's always taking photos.

The picks run in price order, from Under $15 and Under $25 through $25–50 and $50–100. Most work for a teenage sister as well as a grown one with kids of her own.

How we chose these picks

We chose products from brands with long track records and years of steady owner feedback (Mario Badescu, Bananagrams, The Woobles, Revlon, LEGO, Polaroid, Philips) and read the critical reviews so each pick has an honest caveat.

We left out anything that depends on her exact clothing size and focused on things she'll use or enjoy right away.

We list rough price bands instead of exact prices because prices on Amazon change constantly. Tap any button to see today's price. Price mix in this guide: Under $15: 2 · Under $25: 2 · $25–50: 6 · $50–100: 2.

Tips for shopping for your sister

  • Think about the small treat she always talks herself out of buying.
  • A game or craft kit is twice the gift if you set aside time to play or make it together over the holidays.
  • For beauty products, check with her partner or a friend about scents and skin sensitivities.
